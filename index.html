BeatBloom

Compose a beat. Watch it bloom. Share the link.

Click cells to toggle steps. The lead column sets the note - every column is tuned to A minor pentatonic, so anything you draw sounds musical. The whole song lives in the share link: no account, no server.

BeatBloom runs entirely in your browser - Web Audio scheduling, canvas visuals, URL-encoded songs. Built for Next Byte Hacks V5.
